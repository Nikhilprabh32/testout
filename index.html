01 — Who I am

A little about me

Hi, thanks for visiting! My name is Nikhil Prabhu, but I go by Nik. I’ve lived most of my life in Dallas, Texas, and I’m currently a student at Texas A&M University studying finance, information systems, and computer science.

From a very young age, I’ve had an obsession with building things. For me, that started with LEGO. I loved putting things together, taking them apart, and figuring out how they worked. Today, I have a passion for building things that are a little more useful.

I’m extremely interested in the intersection of finance, technology, and entrepreneurship, and I’ve explored those interests through several different ventures. I love building actionable solutions to problems I notice around me and believe there is almost always something deeper than what appears on the surface. You better bet I am going to figure out what that is.

Sports are also a huge part of my life. I grew up playing basketball, football, and soccer, with basketball being my go-to sport. When I am not regularly playing, you can find me watching my Aggies, Cowboys, LA Rams, Lakers, Mavs, and (most recently) 76ers play.

I am always excited to meet new people, builders, sports enthusiasts, (recruiters...), whether that be to connect, hoop, or just discuss our favorite hypotheticals together. Please reach out!!
